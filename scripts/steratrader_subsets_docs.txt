Instruments GET /instruments?group_id=1 groups.read With a group_id , returns that group’s terms per instrument — markup, commission, swap and trade mode. Without one, the full instrument catalogue. Currencies GET /currencies An account is denominated in its own currency, for life. Every amount your integration sees for that account — balance, equity, transactions, deals — is in integer minor units of that currency , and a transaction must be sent in it: a deposit in USD to an NGN account is refused, not silently converted. Margin and P&L on cross-currency instruments are converted by the platform at the broker’s configured rates, and an operation with no rate available is refused rather than guessed. json Copy { "enabled": true, "settlement": "USD", "currencies": ["USD", "NGN", "GHS", "ZAR", "EUR", "GBP"] } Read this once and render a currency picker — or none, when the broker offers a single currency. Which currencies exist and their rates are configured by the broker in their back office. Partner accounts A subset is a partner reselling part of a broker’s book — a prop firm, typically. The subset owns its own account types and tunes them through this API, without a ticket to the broker for every spread change and without reaching the broker’s own retail groups. Your key carries the partner account. Nothing identifies it in a request body, so there is no partner or broker id to send and no way to address another one. Anything outside your own groups answers 404 , never 403 : a 403 would confirm that an id exists, which turns group and account numbers into something worth guessing. Ceilings The broker sets limits you cannot raise from here. They are checked on every write, against your partner account rather than against the request, so a value that exceeds one is refused rather than clamped. max_leverage integer Group leverage and per-instrument overrides are refused above it. min_markup_points number Markup below the floor is refused, on either side of the quote. max_groups integer How many account types you may run. allowed_execution_models string[] Routing stays the broker’s decision. You cannot move your own flow between books. GET /whoami reports the ones that apply to you. To change any of them, ask your broker. Address allow-list A partner key works only from addresses the broker has listed, and a request from anywhere else is refused before it reaches any data. Send your broker the egress addresses of every server that will call this API, and tell them before you move hosts. Your account types GET /groups groups.read PATCH /groups/{group_id} groups.write Send only the fields you are changing. A challenge product usually lives in the leverage, the lot caps and the minimum hold. A group is live or demo, and an account has to match it. An account opened with is_demo set into a live group is refused rather than placed, because the group decides which book the orders reach. json Copy { "leverage": 50, "margin_call": 100, "stop_out": 50, "min_lot": 0.01, "max_lots_per_order": 10, "max_lots_total": 50, "min_hold_seconds": 60, "is_active": true } Stop out has to sit below margin call, or an account is stopped out before it is ever warned. That is validated against the result rather than the request, so sending one field alone still has to land correctly against the value already stored. Spreads and swaps GET /groups/{group_id}/instruments groups.read PATCH /groups/{group_id}/instruments/{instrument_id} instruments.write The read returns every instrument in the group with its symbol, digits and contract size alongside the terms, so you can price from the same numbers the engine quotes from. json Copy { "markup_bid": 8, "markup_ask": 8, "min_spread_points": 0, "commission_per_lot": 3.5, "swap_long": -2.5, "swap_short": -1.8, "trade_mode": "full" } markup_bid, markup_ask number In points, applied to each side of the feed price. Refused below your minimum markup. trade_mode string One of full , close_only or disabled . Use close_only to withdraw an instrument without trapping anyone who is already in it. leverage_override integer Per-instrument leverage, capped by the same ceiling as the group. Moving a trader PATCH /accounts/{login}/group accounts.move Promote a passed evaluation, or move a breached account to a restricted account type. Both groups have to be yours. json Copy { "group": "mff-infinity", "reason": "Evaluation passed" } Refused while positions are open, because the move re-prices their spread, swap and margin mid-trade.
